Install

Add Letter Loom to your home screen

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Letter Loom then opens full screen and works offline.
How to play

Drag across the letters to spell words

Every word in the grid is a construction term. Each one you find fills its row or column. Fill the whole grid to finish the level.

Don't know a word? Tap it in the grid to see it in Russian, Spanish or French.

Stuck? Shuffle the wheel, or spend 30 spools to reveal a letter. Extra words that aren't in the grid fill the bonus meter, and a full meter gives a free hint.
