Install

Add Letter Loom to your home screen

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Letter Loom then opens full screen and works offline.
How to play

Drag across the letters to spell words

Every word you find fills its row or column in the grid. Fill the whole grid to finish the level.

Don't know a word? Tap it in the grid to see it in Russian.

Stuck? Shuffle the wheel, or spend 30 spools to reveal a letter.
